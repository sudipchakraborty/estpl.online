# About Us

## Transforming Vision into Intelligence

ELTERN SEGEN TECHNOLOGIE Pvt. Ltd. is a technology-driven company specializing in Visual AI, intelligent video analytics, industrial automation, and enterprise monitoring solutions. Through our Visual AI platform, **ELVA**, we transform images and video streams into actionable intelligence that helps organizations improve security, operational visibility, process efficiency, quality control, and regulatory compliance.

We combine computer vision, object detection, Optical Character Recognition (OCR), behavioural analytics, anomaly detection, and predictive intelligence to address complex challenges across retail and manufacturing environments. Our solutions are designed not only to detect events but also to verify them, generate real-time alerts, maintain evidence and audit trails, and initiate automated business workflows.

## What We Do

ELVA connects with 4K and AI-enabled IoT cameras, production-line cameras, microscopes, and other imaging devices to continuously observe operational environments. Our AI models analyse the captured information to identify objects, read text, detect anomalies, classify events, and recognise behavioural patterns.

The resulting intelligence is delivered through centralized dashboards, automated alerts, analytical reports, and traceable evidence. Our solutions can also integrate with existing ERP, POS, RFID, barcode, Business Intelligence, and access-control systems, enabling customers to strengthen their current infrastructure without replacing essential business platforms.

## Our Approach

Our Visual AI solutions follow a practical four-stage approach:

**Detect:** Capture and identify objects, activities, defects, text, and operational events using cameras and AI models.

**Verify:** Validate detected events through intelligent classification, evidence capture, business rules, and audit trails.

**Optimize:** Convert visual data into meaningful insights that improve productivity, quality, resource utilization, and decision-making.

**Secure:** Protect people, assets, inventory, processes, and facilities through continuous monitoring and real-time alerts.

Our hybrid edge-and-cloud architecture supports responsive on-site processing while providing centralized monitoring and analytics across multiple locations.
