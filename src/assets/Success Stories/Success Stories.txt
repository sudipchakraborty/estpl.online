## Senco Gold Limited

### AI-Powered Retail Intelligence for Smarter Store Operations

**The Challenge**

A large jewellery retail environment requires continuous visibility across customer movement, sales counters, product displays, staff activity, security events, and connected monitoring devices. Traditional CCTV systems provide recorded footage but cannot automatically transform that footage into meaningful operational insights.

The requirement was to develop an intelligent platform capable of supporting security, customer-service analysis, staff-productivity monitoring, and management-level business intelligence across retail locations.

**Our Solution**

We developed an AI-powered retail intelligence platform designed for the specialized requirements of jewellery stores. The system combines live camera monitoring, computer vision, analytical dashboards, automated alerts, and evidence-based reporting.

The platform supports capabilities such as:

* Customer footfall analysis
* Counter-wise crowd and engagement monitoring
* Queue and waiting-time visibility
* Salesperson activity and idle-time analysis
* Customer and employee sentiment indicators
* Suspicious-behaviour and outsider-alert detection
* Product display and prop monitoring
* Missing-item and display inconsistency alerts
* Camera and IoT device health monitoring
* Multi-location dashboards and evidence management

**The Impact**

The solution enables management teams to move beyond passive CCTV observation and gain structured, actionable insights from store activities. It improves visibility into customer behaviour, staff utilization, store performance, security events, and device availability.

By combining alerts with supporting evidence and analytical dashboards, the platform provides a stronger foundation for informed decision-making, operational review, and continuous improvement across retail locations.

## Gibraltar Airsprings

### Automated Visual Inspection and Traceability for Manufacturing

**The Challenge**

Manufacturing environments depend on accurate identification, inspection, and verification at different stages of production. Manual reading of embossed or printed information can be time-consuming and vulnerable to inconsistencies, particularly when text quality, lighting, surface conditions, or component positioning varies.

The requirement was to build a visual inspection system capable of capturing product information, validating it, and creating a traceable inspection record.

**Our Solution**

We developed an industrial AI inspection platform that combines camera-based image capture, Optical Character Recognition, event validation, and centralized reporting.

The solution supports:

* OCR-based reading of embossed or printed information
* Expected-versus-detected text verification
* Pass-and-fail classification
* Confidence-based inspection results
* Camera-wise live monitoring
* Evidence-image capture and storage
* Event history and traceability
* Inspection dashboards and reports
* Real-time communication between edge processing and the central platform

The system was designed with a modular architecture so that additional inspection capabilities, such as surface-defect detection, barcode verification, and product-quality validation, can be integrated as requirements evolve.

**The Impact**

The platform creates a structured and auditable inspection process by connecting every detected event with its result, confidence level, timestamp, camera source, and supporting evidence.

This approach reduces dependence on unstructured manual observation, improves inspection consistency, and gives production and quality teams clearer visibility into manufacturing events. Its modular design also provides a scalable foundation for future AI-driven inspection workflows.

## Dhunseri Polyfilms Pvt. Limited

### Visual AI for Manufacturing Visibility and Process Control

**The Challenge**

Modern manufacturing facilities require consistent monitoring across production, packing, dispatch, worker safety, and quality-control activities. Manual supervision alone may not provide continuous visibility or timely identification of operational deviations.

The objective was to improve plant-level awareness by using camera-based intelligence to identify relevant events and present them through a centralized monitoring system.

**Our Solution**

We applied Visual AI and intelligent monitoring capabilities to support manufacturing analytics and operational visibility. The solution framework was designed to process camera feeds, identify configured activities or anomalies, and generate structured information for review.

The platform supports use cases such as:

* Production-area monitoring
* Pre-Dispatch Inspection support
* Packing and dispatch verification
* Workplace safety and PPE-compliance monitoring
* Intrusion and restricted-area detection
* Process-event identification
* Camera and device health visibility
* Automated alerts with supporting evidence
* Centralized dashboards for operational review

**The Impact**

The solution gives operational teams a more consistent view of activities across monitored areas. Automated detection and evidence capture help relevant personnel review incidents more efficiently and respond to exceptions with better context.

By bringing multiple Visual AI capabilities into a common platform, the system supports improved traceability, stronger compliance visibility, and more informed production oversight.
